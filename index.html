FAQs

What is a pomodoro?

A pomodoro is a time management technique that uses a timer to break down work into intervals, traditionally 25 minutes in length, separated by short breaks.

How does it help with productivity?

The pomodoro technique helps with productivity by creating a structured approach to work, reducing procrastination, and improving focus.

What are all the noises for?

The different noises are designed to create a more immersive environment, helping you concentrate better and block out distractions.

How do I reset my stats?

You can reset your stats at any time by clicking the "Reset Stats" button in the settings menu.
